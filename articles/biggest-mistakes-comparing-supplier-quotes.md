# The Biggest Mistakes Buyers Make When Comparing Supplier Quotes

Choosing the lowest supplier quote is not always the smartest procurement decision.

This research explores the most common mistakes buyers make when evaluating supplier quotations and explains how professional procurement teams compare offers using market benchmarks, commercial terms, supplier credibility, and total landed cost.

## Key Takeaways

- Don't compare prices without market benchmarks.
- Consider total landed cost instead of quoted price.
- Verify supplier credibility before awarding contracts.
- Commercial terms can outweigh price differences.
- Structured comparison reduces procurement risk.
