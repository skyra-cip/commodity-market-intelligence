# 7 Hidden Costs That Make Commodity Procurement More Expensive

Most procurement teams focus on supplier quotations.

Professional buyers focus on total acquisition cost.

This research explains seven hidden cost factors that influence commodity procurement, including logistics, financing, verification, inventory, supplier risk, currency exposure, and market timing.

## Topics

- Commodity Procurement
- Procurement Intelligence
- Commodity Pricing
- Supply Chain
- Supplier Evaluation
- Risk Management
- Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

## Key Takeaways

- The cheapest quote is rarely the lowest-cost transaction.
- Logistics and financing significantly affect procurement economics.
- Supplier verification reduces execution risk.
- Procurement decisions should combine pricing with market intelligence.
- Total Cost of Ownership provides a more accurate decision framework.
