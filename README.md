30| 
31| **Published:** August 10, 2026 | **Author:** Skyra CIP – Commodity Market Intelligence
32| 
33| Commodity prices can remain unchanged while procurement costs increase. Why? Because many globally traded commodities are priced in USD, while buyers operate in other currencies.
34| 
35| #### Key Takeaways
36| 
37| - 🔹 **True Procurement Cost is more than the supplier's price** - It includes commodity price, premium/discount, freight, financing, FX impact, and other transaction costs
38| - 🔹 **A weaker buyer currency materially changes transaction economics** - Supplier price doesn't need to increase for your cost to rise
39| - 🔹 **Professional procurement teams monitor FX as closely as commodity prices** - Currency movements are cost factors
40| - 🔹 **Payment timing and financing costs amplify FX exposure** - Transaction structure matters
41|
