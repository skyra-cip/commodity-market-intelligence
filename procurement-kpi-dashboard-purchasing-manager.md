# The Procurement KPI Dashboard Every Purchasing Manager Should Have

Procurement performance cannot be measured by price alone.

A practical KPI dashboard should connect:

- Market-adjusted savings
- Purchase price variance
- Total procurement cost
- Supplier price performance
- Freight cost
- On-time delivery
- Supplier fill rate
- Contract coverage
- Open market exposure
- FX exposure
- Supplier concentration
- PO cycle time
- Savings realization

The objective is simple:

Turn procurement data into better commercial decisions.

A good dashboard should answer three questions:

1. What changed?
2. Why did it change?
3. What should procurement do next?

## Core Principle

Procurement KPIs should measure outcomes, not simply activity.

The right dashboard connects:

Market → Supplier → Cost → Risk → Decision
