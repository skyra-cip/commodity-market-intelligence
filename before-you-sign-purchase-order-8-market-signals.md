# Before You Sign a Purchase Order, Check These 8 Market Signals

A purchase order is more than a commercial document.

In commodity markets, it is a commitment to a market view.

Before signing, procurement teams should evaluate:

1. Market Benchmark
2. Premium / Discount
3. Inventory & Physical Availability
4. Futures Curve
5. Freight & Logistics
6. Currency & Financing
7. Supply & Geopolitical Risk
8. Refining Margins & Product Economics

## Core Principle

Don't look at the supplier's price in isolation.

Understand the market behind the price.

## Procurement Framework

Market Data  
→ Benchmark  
→ Physical Conditions  
→ Supplier Quote  
→ Risk  
→ Total Cost  
→ Procurement Decision

## Key Takeaway

The smartest procurement teams don't try to predict every market move.

They build a framework that helps them understand whether a quotation is competitive, expensive, misleading, or simply not comparable.
