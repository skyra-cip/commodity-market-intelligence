# Why Two Suppliers Can Offer Completely Different Prices for the Same Product

Two suppliers can offer the same commodity at significantly different prices.

But the headline price rarely tells the complete story.

Supplier quotations can differ because of:

- Market benchmarks
- Product specifications
- Incoterms
- Freight
- Financing
- Payment terms
- Supplier margins
- Inventory position
- Market timing
- Counterparty risk

## The Core Principle

The cheapest quotation is not always the cheapest transaction.

Professional procurement teams compare:

Market Price
+ Logistics
+ Financing
+ Quality
+ Risk
+ Timing
= True Commercial Value

## Key Takeaway

Don't ask only:

"Who offers the lowest price?"

Ask:

"What is behind the price difference?"
